MAN Enterprise is invited to communicate its decision within five (5) business days of the demo. 2. AI Engine — Core Capabilities Included

  • Adaptive Learning Loop: The engine identifies team refinements and remembers them across future generations for continuous compounding output accuracy.
  • Calibrated System Instructions: Operating instructions are specifically customized to MAN’s engineering vocabulary, tone, and BOQ conventions at inception.
  • Automatic Template Continuity: Dynamic alignment with complex branded Word templates (margins, letterhead, tables) refining progressively.
  • Corpus Q&A & Knowledge Capture: Instant natural-language querying of MAN's private project archives with explicit source citations.
